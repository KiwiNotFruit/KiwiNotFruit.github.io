RECOIL CALCULATION FORMULA

Stats needed:
- Impulse_Primary (total kick force)
- ImpulseZOnly_Primary (vertical component)
- RecoilRecoveryTime_Primary (how fast it settles)

Formula:
recoilImpactScore = (Impulse_Primary + ImpulseZOnly_Primary) / RecoilRecoveryTime_Primary

Rating Scale:
- Very Low:  < 10,000
- Low:       10,000 - 20,000
- Moderate:  20,000 - 40,000
- High:      40,000 - 60,000
- Very High: > 60,000

ACCURACY CALCULATION FORMULA

Stats needed:
- MinimumSpreadX/Y_Primary (base spread)
- MaximumSpreadX/Y_Primary (max spread)
- SpreadIncreasePerShotX/Y_Primary (bloom per shot)
- SpreadDecayX/Y_Primary (recovery rate)

Formula:
1. Average X and Y values for each stat
2. bloom_speed = SpreadIncrease × 10 (shots per second)
3. recovery_rate = SpreadDecay (or 0.1 if zero)
4. accuracyScore = (MinSpread + MaxSpread) + (bloom_speed / recovery_rate)

Rating Scale:
- Excellent: < 11.5 (tight, stable, minimal bloom)
- Good:      11.5 - 23.0 (controlled spread)
- Neutral:   23.0 - 34.5 (moderate spread, manageable bloom)
- Poor:      34.5 - 46.0 (significant bloom, loose)
- Very Poor: > 46.0 (extreme spread, hard to control)
